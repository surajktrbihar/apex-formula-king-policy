Apex Formula King Privacy Policy

Publisher: apex education

Study activity

The app does not require a study account. The app stores answers, progress, and preferences on the device. You can remove them by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling it. Android backup settings may affect whether device data is backed up.

Advertising and app configuration

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to retrieve feature settings and may show banner or interstitial advertising through Google AdMob. Firebase may process an installation identifier and technical information needed to deliver configuration. When advertising is enabled, Google may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Google’s consent tools present applicable choices. When required, you can revisit them using Ad privacy choices in the app.
